# Funding & Sponsorship Opportunities

## Executive Summary

NostroQ is positioned at the intersection of **fintech** and **quantum computing** - two areas with active funding and sponsorship programs. This document identifies specific funding sources available today, even without paying customers.

## 3. Pitch Positioning by Audience

### For Quantum Computing Programs

**Lead with**:
- Novel QUBO formulation for liquidity optimization
- Real-world financial use case with measurable impact
- Clear path from simulation to hardware

**Avoid**:
- Overstating current quantum advantage
- Vague "quantum finance" claims

### For Fintech Programs/VCs

**Lead with**:
- $X billion market for treasury optimization
- Capital efficiency gains (20%+ reduction in idle capital)
- Clear unit economics and go-to-market

**Avoid**:
- Leading with quantum (save for differentiation)
- Technical details before business case

### For Bank Innovation Labs

**Lead with**:
- Understanding of their specific treasury challenges
- Shadow mode = zero risk to try
- Compliance/audit-ready from day one

**Avoid**:
- Quantum hype (banks are skeptical)
- Asking for production access too early

### For Government Grants

**Lead with**:
- Alignment with National Quantum Mission
- India-first opportunity (GIFT City)
- Academic collaboration potential

**Avoid**:
- Pure commercial framing
- Timelines that depend on uncertain quantum progress
