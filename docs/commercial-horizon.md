# Commercial Horizon Analysis

## Executive Summary

NostroQ targets treasury teams at banks and payment institutions operating cross-border corridors. The path to commercial viability follows a clear progression from **decision support** to **quantum-enhanced optimization**, with revenue beginning in the classical-only phase.

---

## 1. Market Opportunity

### The Problem We Solve

Banks operating cross-border payment corridors must pre-fund nostro accounts to guarantee settlement. The conservative default is **over-provisioning** - holding excess capital that earns no return.

**Quantified Impact (Illustrative)**:
- A mid-size bank with 10 corridors might hold $500M in nostro balances
- If 20% ($100M) is excess capital
- At 5% cost of capital = **$5M annual opportunity cost**

### Target Market

| Segment | Characteristics | Estimated Size |
|---------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Tier 1 Banks** | 50+ corridors, $1B+ nostro | 50-100 globally |
| **Tier 2 Banks** | 10-50 corridors, $100M-$1B nostro | 500-1,000 globally |
| **Payment Institutions** | 5-20 corridors, $50M-$500M | 200-500 globally |
| **GIFT City Entities** | New IFSC operations | 50+ and growing |

### Why Now?

1. **Interest Rate Environment**: Higher rates make idle capital more expensive
2. **Regulatory Pressure**: Basel IV increases capital efficiency focus
3. **Technology Readiness**: Classical optimization mature, quantum emerging
4. **GIFT City Opportunity**: New regulatory sandbox encouraging innovation

## 2. Business Model

### Pricing Philosophy

NostroQ is a **software product, not a consulting service**. Pricing scales with usage, not hours.

### Revenue Model Options

#### Option A: Per-Corridor Pricing

| Tier | Corridors | Monthly Price | Annual |
|------|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Starter | 1-5 | $5,000 | $60,000 |
| Growth | 6-20 | $15,000 | $180,000 |
| Enterprise | 21-50 | $35,000 | $420,000 |
| Network | 50+ | Custom | $500K+ |

#### Option B: AUM-Based Pricing

| Total Nostro AUM | Annual Fee (bps) | Example |
|------------------|------------------|---------|
| <$100M | 5 bps | $50K on $100M |
| $100M-$500M | 3 bps | $150K on $500M |
| $500M-$1B | 2 bps | $200K on $1B |
| >$1B | 1.5 bps | $300K on $2B |

#### Option C: Value-Based Pricing

- **Base Fee**: $50,000/year for platform access
- **Success Fee**: 10% of documented savings above baseline
- **Example**: If system saves $2M/year, fee = $50K + $200K = $250K

### Quantum Premium (Future)

When quantum hardware provides measurable advantage:
- **Standard Tier**: Classical optimization only
- **Quantum Tier**: +50% premium for quantum-enhanced optimization
- **Exclusive Tier**: Priority quantum hardware access, custom SLAs
